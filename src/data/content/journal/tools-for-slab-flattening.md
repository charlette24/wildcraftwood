A router sled can flatten slabs that exceed the capacity of a jointer or planer, but the quality of the result depends on the entire system.

## The system matters more than one tool

Evaluate the router, flattening bit, rails, sled rigidity, dust collection, slab support, and workholding as a single setup.

## Prioritize control

Use conservative passes, verify that rails are coplanar, and prevent the slab from shifting. Protective equipment and effective dust collection are essential.
